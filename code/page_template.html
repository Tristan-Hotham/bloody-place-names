Bloody place names

Britain's map is scattered with Battle, Bloody Meadow, Slaughter, Dead Man's Hill and Gallows Hill. Do these names mark the sites of real fights, or are most of them red herrings? I measured {{n_matched_features|int}} of them against every recorded battle and siege I could place.
